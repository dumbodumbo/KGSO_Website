About KGSO

Who we are

What it is

The KGS STEM Society is a student-run organization at Karachi Grammar School College Section, rooted in the school's long-running science and math societies, including the Einstein Society. Its flagship is the Karachi Grammar School Olympiad (KGSO), a multi-day national STEM competition.

The competition

The 13th annual edition runs in 2027, twelve years of KGSO. It is one of the largest high school STEM competitions in Pakistan. It draws A-Level schools, O-Level institutions, and colleges nationwide. 500+ delegates take part. Teams represent schools or compete as independent external delegations.
